ScribeBox Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026

In short: ScribeBox records, transcribes, identifies speakers and writes summaries entirely on your phone. Your audio, transcripts and summaries are never uploaded, and we (the developer) receive none of your data. The free version shows ads from Google, and Google collects some device data to show and measure those ads. If you buy “Remove ads”, ScribeBox does not use the network at all.

Who we are

ScribeBox is developed by tethered.systems (“we”, “us”). Contact: tethered.systems@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you create in ScribeBox is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • audio recordings, and audio decoded from files you import;
  • transcripts, speaker names and voice profiles (numerical “voice fingerprints” used to recognise a speaker, not audio);
  • summaries, action items and your edits;
  • the speech and language models you import, and your settings.

Speech recognition, speaker identification and summarisation run on the device. None of this content is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else. ScribeBox has no account, no cloud sync and no cloud backup (Android backup is turned off for the app). If you choose Live transcribe, the audio is deleted as soon as processing finishes and only the text is kept.

Content leaves your phone only when you share an export (for example to email or a messaging app); what happens then is governed by the app you share it with.

What we collect

Nothing. ScribeBox contains no analytics, crash reporting or tracking code of our own, and we operate no servers.

Ads in the free version (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner on the home screen and, at most every few minutes, a full-screen ad after you stop a recording. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention:

DataExamples
Device or other identifiersAndroid advertising ID, app set ID
Approximate locationEstimated from your IP address
App interactionsApp launches, taps on ads, video views
DiagnosticsApp performance such as launch time

This data is encrypted in transit. The ads code never has access to your recordings, transcripts or summaries. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent message asks for your choice before personalised ads are shown; you can change it later in Settings → Privacy choices for ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy (or Google) → Ads. How Google uses this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

“Remove ads” and payments

“Remove ads” is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The app asks Google Play only whether the purchase was made (Google Play may contact its servers to answer, as for any Play purchase). Once ads are removed, the ads SDK is never started and ScribeBox makes no network connections.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your content stays until you delete it. Deleting a meeting removes its audio, transcript, speakers and summary from the device. Uninstalling ScribeBox removes all of its data. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on request; for data collected by Google for ads, see Google's controls linked above.

Children

ScribeBox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect anything from them (we collect nothing from anyone).

Security

Your content is kept in Android's app-private storage, which other apps cannot read. Keep your phone locked to protect it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date, and describe significant changes in the app's release notes.
